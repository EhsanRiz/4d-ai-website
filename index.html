Research Collaboration · University of Virginia

AI for Agricultural Resilience — UVA × 4D Decision Support System

With faculty at the University of Virginia and the National University of Lesotho, 4D is piloting a retrieval-augmented (RAG) AI Decision Support System that turns ReNOKA's 500-page Compendium of soil and water conservation measures into real-time, actionable guidance for extension agents and farmers — continuously improved through expert-in-the-loop feedback, with Sesotho-language support planned.

17 extension agents · 340 farmers Two planting seasons, 2026–2027
